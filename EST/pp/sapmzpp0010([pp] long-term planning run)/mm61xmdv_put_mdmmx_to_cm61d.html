*--> Daten sichern

  MOVE MDMMX-PLWRK TO CM61D-WERKS.
  MOVE MDMMX-BERID TO CM61D-BERID.
  MOVE MDMMX-DISST TO CM61D-DISST.
  MOVE MDMMX-RESDT TO CM61D-RESDT.
  MOVE MDMMX-RESAL TO CM61D-RESAL.
  MOVE MDMMX-RHYTD TO CM61D-RHYTD.
  MOVE MDMMX-PARMX TO CM61D-PARMX.     "parallel innerhalb
  MOVE MDMMX-PARCT TO CM61D-PARCT.     "KdE-Material
