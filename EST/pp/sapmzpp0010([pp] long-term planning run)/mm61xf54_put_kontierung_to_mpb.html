Program listing for: MM61XF54_PUT_KONTIERUNG_TO_MPB 
*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
* Kontierung nach MPBA transportieren                                  *
*----------------------------------------------------------------------*


FORM PUT_KONTIERUNG_TO_MPBA.
*--> Pruefen Stern-EINGABE
  IF RM61X-KNTTP EQ STERN.
    PERFORM PUT_MPBA_KNTTP.
    IF MPBA-VBELN IS INITIAL
    OR MPBA-VBELP IS INITIAL.
      PERFORM CLEAR_KNTTP.
    ENDIF.
    IF  MPBA-KZVBR EQ KZVBRP
    AND MPBA-PSPEL IS INITIAL.
      PERFORM CLEAR_KNTTP.
    ENDIF.
    IF  MPBA-KZVBR EQ KZVBRE.
      CLEAR RM61X-PSPEL.
    ENDIF.
  ENDIF.
*--> Kontierungsdaten zuruecktransportieren
  MOVE RM61X-KNTTP TO MPBA-KNTTP.
  MOVE RM61X-SOBKZ TO MPBA-SOBKZ.
  MOVE RM61X-KZVBR TO MPBA-KZVBR.
  MOVE RM61X-KZBWS TO MPBA-KZBWS.
  MOVE RM61X-KDAUF TO MPBA-VBELN.
  MOVE RM61X-KDPOS TO MPBA-VBELP.
  MOVE RM61X-PSPEL TO MPBA-PSPEL.
  MOVE RM61X-CUOBJ TO MPBA-CUOBJ.
  MOVE RM61X-TECHS TO MPBA-TECHS.
ENDFORM.
