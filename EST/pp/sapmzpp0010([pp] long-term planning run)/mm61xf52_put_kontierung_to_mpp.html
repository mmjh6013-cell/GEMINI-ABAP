Program listing for: MM61XF52_PUT_KONTIERUNG_TO_MPP 
*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
* Kontierung nach MPPA transportieren                                  *
*----------------------------------------------------------------------*


FORM PUT_KONTIERUNG_TO_MPPA.
*--> Pruefen Stern-EINGABE
  IF RM61X-KNTTP EQ STERN.
    PERFORM PUT_MPPA_KNTTP.
    IF MPPA-KDAUF IS INITIAL
    OR MPPA-KDPOS IS INITIAL.
      PERFORM CLEAR_KNTTP.
    ENDIF.
    IF  MPPA-KZVBR EQ KZVBRP
    AND MPPA-PSPEL IS INITIAL.
      PERFORM CLEAR_KNTTP.
    ENDIF.
    IF  MPPA-KZVBR EQ KZVBRE.
      CLEAR RM61X-PSPEL.
    ENDIF.
  ENDIF.
*--> Pruefen Kontierungsdaten
  MOVE RM61X-KNTTP TO MPPA-KNTTP.
  MOVE RM61X-SOBKZ TO MPPA-SOBKZ.
  MOVE RM61X-KZVBR TO MPPA-KZVBR.
  MOVE RM61X-KZBWS TO MPPA-KZBWS.
  MOVE RM61X-KDAUF TO MPPA-KDAUF.
  MOVE RM61X-KDPOS TO MPPA-KDPOS.
  MOVE RM61X-PSPEL TO MPPA-PSPEL.
  MOVE RM61X-CUOBJ TO MPPA-CUOBJ.
  MOVE RM61X-TECHS TO MPPA-TECHS.
*--> Pruefen Konfigurationsdaten
  IF MT61D-KZKFG EQ XFLAG.
    IF RM61X-CUOBJ IS INITIAL.
      MOVE MT61D-CUOBJ TO RM61X-CUOBJ.
    ENDIF.
  ENDIF.
ENDFORM.
