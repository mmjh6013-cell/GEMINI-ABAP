Program listing for: MM61XF56_MOVE_MPLP_TO_MDPS 
*----------------------------------------------------------------------*
* MPLP nach MDPS uebernehmen
*----------------------------------------------------------------------*


FORM MOVE_MPLP_TO_MDPS.
*--> MDPS zuruecksetzen
  CLEAR MDPS.
*--> Sortierblock setzen
  MOVE NETPLA     TO MDPS-PLAAB.
  MOVE S1PLUSX    TO MDPS-SORT1.
  MOVE S2LPEIN    TO MDPS-SORT2.
*--> Pruefen Bruttoplanung
  IF MT61D-MISKZ EQ MISKZB.
    MOVE BRTPLA     TO MDPS-PLAAB.
  ENDIF.
*--> Pruefen Lagerortdisposition
  IF MDPS-PLAAB EQ NETPLA
  OR MDPS-PLAAB EQ BRTPLA.
    IF NOT MPLP-LGORT IS INITIAL.
      PERFORM CHECK_LGORT USING MPLP-LGORT.
      IF MARD-DISKZ EQ DISKZ1.      "Lagerort ausgeschlossen
        MOVE LGODS2     TO MDPS-PLAAB.
        MOVE MPLP-LGORT TO PLALG-LGORT.
        MOVE PLALG      TO MDPS-PLANR.
      ENDIF.
      IF MARD-DISKZ EQ DISKZ2.      "Lagerort seperat disponieren
        MOVE LGODS1     TO MDPS-PLAAB.
        MOVE MPLP-LGORT TO PLALG-LGORT.
        MOVE PLALG      TO MDPS-PLANR.
      ENDIF.
    ENDIF.
  ENDIF.
*--> Daten uebernehmen
  MOVE LPEIN      TO MDPS-DELKZ.
  MOVE PLUSX      TO MDPS-PLUMI.
  MOVE XFLAG      TO MDPS-VRFKZ.
  MOVE BESKZF     TO MDPS-BESKZ.
  MOVE MPLP-EBELN TO MDPS-DELNR.
  MOVE MPLP-EBELP TO MDPS-DELPS.
  MOVE MPLP-ETENR TO MDPS-DELET.
  MOVE MPLP-PSTYP TO MDPS-SOBES.
  IF MDPS-SOBES EQ SOBESU.                                "note 650227
    MOVE MPLP-RESWK TO MDPS-WRK02.                        "note 650227
  ENDIF.
  MOVE MPLP-EINDT TO MDPS-DAT01.
*--> Bei Lohnbearbeitung auch DAT02 neu ermitteln         P30K101124 JB
  IF MPLP-PSTYP EQ '3'.
     PERFORM READ_MDRV USING MPLP-EBELN MPLP-EBELP.
     IF NOT MDRV-PLIFZ IS INITIAL.    "DAT02 mit Lieferzeit aus Vertrag
        PERFORM DAT02_AUS_DAT01_MPLP USING    MDRV-PLIFZ
                                              MPLP-EINDT
                                     CHANGING MDPS-DAT02.
     ELSE.                           "DAT02 mit Lieferzeit aus Matstamm
        PERFORM DAT02_AUS_DAT01_MPLP USING    MT61D-PLIFZ
                                              MPLP-EINDT
                                     CHANGING MDPS-DAT02.
     ENDIF.
  ENDIF.

  MOVE MPLP-KNTTP TO MDPS-KNTTP.
  MOVE MPLP-SOBKZ TO MDPS-SOBKZ.
  MOVE MPLP-KZVBR TO MDPS-KZVBR.
  MOVE mplp-kzbws TO mdps-kzbws.                         "HW 334232
  MOVE MPLP-VBELN TO MDPS-KDAUF.
  MOVE MPLP-VBELP TO MDPS-KDPOS.
  MOVE MPLP-PSPEL TO MDPS-PSPEL.
  MOVE MPLP-RSNUM TO MDPS-RSNUM.
  MOVE MPLP-AKTAL TO MDPS-AKTAL.
  MOVE MPLP-VERID TO MDPS-VERID.
  MOVE MPLP-CUOBJ TO MDPS-CUOBJ.
* MOVE mplp-techs TO mdps-techs.
  MOVE MPLP-BSART TO MDPS-BAART.
  MOVE MPLP-KZBWS TO MDPS-KZBWS.
  MOVE MPLP-REVLV TO MDPS-REVLV.

  MOVE MPLP-WEBAZ TO MDPS-WEBAZ.
  MOVE MPLP-LGORT TO MDPS-LGORT.
  MOVE MPLP-MENGE TO MDPS-MNG01.
  MOVE MPLP-MENGE TO MDPS-ETMEN.                           "HW 410754
  MOVE MPLP-SERNR TO MDPS-SERNR.
  MOVE MPLP-FIX01 TO MDPS-FIX01.
  MOVE MPLP-QUNUM TO MDPS-QUNUM.
  MOVE MPLP-QUPOS TO MDPS-QUPOS.
  MOVE MPLP-FLIEF TO MDPS-LIFNR.                           "HW 171004
*--> Daten aus OLD-Dispositionszeile
  IF AM61X-VSTAT EQ VSTATV.
    MOVE MDPSS-VSTAT TO MDPS-VSTAT.
    MOVE MDPSS-AUSSL TO MDPS-AUSSL.
    MOVE MDPSS-OLDSL TO MDPS-OLDSL.
    MOVE MDPSS-RSNUM TO MDPS-RSNUM.
    MOVE MDPSS-MNG03 TO MDPS-MNG03.
    MOVE MDPSS-BESKZ TO MDPS-BESKZ.
    IF MDPSX-SOBES NE SOBESU.
      MOVE MDPSS-WRK02 TO MDPS-WRK02.
    ENDIF.
    MOVE MDPSS-BEDAT TO MDPS-BEDAT.
    MOVE MDPSS-GLMNG TO MDPS-GLMNG.
    MOVE MDPSS-LIFNR TO MDPS-LIFNR.                        "HW 171004
    MOVE MDPSS-RESLO TO MDPS-RESLO.                        "SSLOC
  ENDIF.
*--> Reset Daten in OLD-Workaerea
  IF AM61X-VSTAT EQ VSTATV.
    CLEAR MDPSS-UKZ01.
    CLEAR MDPSS-UMDAT.
    CLEAR MDPSS-DAT02.
    CLEAR MDPSS-DAT03.
    CLEAR MDPSS-MNG02.
    CLEAR MDPSS-MNG04.
  ENDIF.
ENDFORM.
