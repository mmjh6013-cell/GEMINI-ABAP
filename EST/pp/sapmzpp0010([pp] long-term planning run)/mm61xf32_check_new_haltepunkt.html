FORM CHECK_NEW_HALTEPUNKT.
*--> Abbruchbedingung pruefen
  IF CM61X-ANZDL NE XFLAG.
    EXIT.
  ENDIF.
*--> Haltepunkt pruefen
  IF  RM61X-MATNR IS INITIAL
  AND RM61X-WERKS IS INITIAL
  AND RM61X-DISST IS INITIAL
  AND RM61X-BERID IS INITIAL.
*--> Naechsten Haltepunkt vorschlagen
    MOVE CM61D-DISST TO RM61X-DISST.
    MOVE CM61D-MATNR TO RM61X-MATNR.
    MOVE CM61D-WERKS TO RM61X-WERKS.
    MOVE CM61D-BERID TO RM61X-BERID.
*--> Call POP-UP
    CALL SCREEN 0155
         STARTING AT 1  9.
  ENDIF.
ENDFORM.
